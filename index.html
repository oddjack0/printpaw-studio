FAQ

What file formats do I get?

Calendars, planners and wall art are delivered as print-ready PDF files (with high-resolution artwork), and design singles are high-resolution image files. The Morbid Funny Quotes bundle is delivered as SVG files for cutting machines and design software.

How do I print my downloads?

Print at 100% scale (turn off “fit to page”) on US Letter or A4. For the best results use “Best” quality, matte photo paper or cardstock for wall art, and standard paper for planner pages. Colors can vary slightly between printers. You can also take the files to any local or online print shop.

What can I use my printables for?

Everything in the shop is licensed for personal use: print for yourself, your home, your planner, or as a gift. Please don't resell, redistribute or share the digital files, or sell printed copies. If you'd like to use a design commercially, contact us through Ko-fi first.

How and when do I receive my files?

Immediately after checkout on Ko-fi — the download link appears on the confirmation screen and is emailed to you. If a download link ever expires, Ko-fi support or the shop messages can get you sorted.

Do you offer refunds?

Because these are instant digital downloads, completed purchases generally can't be returned. If a file is broken or the wrong item, message the shop on Ko-fi and we'll make it right with a fixed file or replacement.

Where else can I find these designs?

Many designs are also available as printed merch — shirts, stickers, mugs and more — in our Redbubble shop, professionally printed and shipped to you.
